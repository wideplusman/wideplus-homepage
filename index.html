First project

국토탐사맵 — 부동산 정보를
더 투명하게, 더 제대로

한국의 부동산 정보는 아직 심하게 한쪽으로 치우쳐 있고, 부동산 서비스는 시장의 크기만큼 앞서 있지 못합니다. 국토탐사맵은 열린 마음으로 정보를 투명하게 나누고, 제대로 된 분석과 컨설팅으로 좁은 대한민국의 국토와 부동산을 더 효율적으로 쓰게 만들려고 합니다.

In English

Wideplus, founded in 2022 in Korea, works under one idea: share creativity, grow together — people building freely together, sharing the results, so that more people benefit. We started in real estate but are not limited to it, and plan open, collaborative projects such as a DAO. Our first project, Gukto Tamsa Map (국토탐사맵), tackles the information gap in Korean real estate: it brings parcels, transactions, permits and redevelopment zones onto one map, answers practical questions such as whether a small residential (villa) project is feasible, and is meant as an open tool that brokers, developers and consultants can build better services on. It is built with Claude Code by a non-developer founder leading a team of AI agents. Currently preparing for beta.
